type: card
name: Standard Tuning
intro: Fine tuning to standard pitch (recommended every 6-12 months). Adjusts for changes due to seasonal humidity and playing. It may include a minor pitch correction if one section of the piano is further out of tune/off pitch.

---

type: card
name: Standard Tuning with Pitch Correction
intro: A full pitch correction before the fine tuning. Appropriate and necessary if:
bullets:
- your piano has not been tuned in a year or so
-if I find the pitch is off substantially from standard (A-440)
If you are unsure whether your piano needs this, choose the Standard Tuning above and I will assess the pitch when I visit.

---

type: card
name: Repairs (on site)
intro: Common repairs I can take care of on site include keys slow to return, notes that continue to sound, missing keytops, broken strings, pedal issues, clicks and pops, etc. Small jobs can sometimes be done during a tuning. If your piano has not been tuned in more than 6 months, I recommend scheduling a tuning at the same time.

---

type: card
name: Regulation (Coming soon)
intro: Adjusts and lubricates many parts to restore high function and playability. Addresses wear, friction, and humidity-related issues. Recommended at least every five years (more often in the first five years or if played more) or if it responds poorly, feels uncomfortable to play, sounds dull or uneven in volume, or makes clicking, popping, or other odd sounds.

---

type: card
name: Basic Cleaning*
intro: Includes thorough cleaning of keytops, dusting and vacuuming the soundboard and tuning pin area, and wiping down the outer case. (*Completed at time of tuning) 

---

type: card
name: Full Cleaning
intro: Includes elements in a Basic Cleaning plus cleaning the “insides”: the action and action cavity, keyframe, underneath the keys, and the key pins. Regular cleaning of these areas helps keep your piano working well and helps prevent deteriorating function and damage.

---

type: card
name: Inspection and Written Report
intro: I examine the condition parts and function and provide a report with photos afterwards. Report also provides recommended work for improving playability and sound quality. This is not an appraisal for insurance or for an expected price of a sale. A tuning is recommended and typically scheduled at the same time.
