intro >>>
Tuning is recommended twice a year for people who play regularly. If that’s not financially feasible, tuning at least once a year can help your piano’s tuning begin to stay stable longer and helps avoid or address problems early.

If your piano is not working right or doesn’t feel or sound right, I recommend booking a tuning and I can troubleshoot and offer repair or regulation options. Let me know as descriptively as you can what’s happening with your piano when you schedule or contact me.

Not sure what you need? Book a standard tuning for now, or [Contact me](/contact) any time.

type: card
name: Standard Tuning
intro: Fine tuning to standard pitch (recommended every 6-12 months). Adjusts for changes due to seasonal humidity and playing. It may include a minor pitch correction if one section of the piano is further out of tune/off pitch.

---

type: card
name: Standard Tuning with Pitch Correction
intro: A full pitch correction before the fine tuning. If you are unsure whether your piano needs this, choose the Standard Tuning above and I will assess the pitch when I visit. Appropriate and often necessary if:
bullets:
- your piano has not been tuned in more than a year
- if I find the pitch is off substantially from standard (A-440)

---

type: card
name: Repairs (on site)
intro: Common repairs I can take care of on site: keys slow to return, notes that continue to sound, missing keytops, broken strings, pedal issues, clicks and pops, and more. Small jobs can sometimes be done during a tuning. If your piano has not been tuned in more than 6 months, I recommend scheduling a tuning at the same time.

---

type: card
name: Regulation (Coming soon)
intro: Adjusts and lubricates many parts to restore high function and smooth playing. Addresses wear, friction, and humidity-related issues. Recommended at least every five years (more often in the first five years or if played more) or if it responds poorly, feels uncomfortable to play, sounds dull or uneven in volume, or makes clicking, popping, or other odd sounds.

---

type: card
name: Basic Cleaning*
intro: Includes thorough cleaning of keytops, dusting and vacuuming the soundboard and tuning pin area, and wiping down the outer case. (*Completed at time of tuning) 

---

type: card
name: Full Cleaning
intro: Includes elements in a Basic Cleaning plus cleaning the “inside”: the action and action cavity, keyframe, underneath the keys, and the key pins. Regular cleaning of these areas helps keep your piano working well and helps prevent deteriorating function and damage.

---

type: card
name: Inspection and Written Report
intro: I examine the condition of its parts and function and provide a report with photos afterwards. Report also provides recommended work for improving how it plays and sound quality. This is not an appraisal for insurance or for an expected price of a sale but is often used for selling or purchasing. A tuning is recommended and typically scheduled at the same time.

I’m a full-time professional piano technician / tuner living in Easthampton and serving Hampshire, Hampden, and parts of Franklin Counties and nearby northern Connecticut. Whether your piano is an upright or a grand, I will give it my full attention and care. 

I was trained at North Bennet Street School in Boston, the only full-time, in-person program of its kind in the country. I am also a member of the Piano Technicians Guild. 

The piano is an incredible and complex instrument. The great pleasure of my work is to make yours sound beautiful and to make playing it more enjoyable and satisfying. It’s rewarding work, and meeting piano owners and players is the bonus. I look forward to meeting you and getting to work on your piano.
